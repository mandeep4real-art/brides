    Fit & sizing

    Measure once, alter less

    Measure over the underwear you plan to wear on the day. Use a soft tape and keep it level. Every piece has a 1.5″ seam allowance at the side seams.

    Bust
    Around the fullest part of the chest, tape level across the back.
    Underbust
    Directly under the bust, snug but not tight. Needed for lehenga blouses and corsetry.
    Waist
    At the natural waist, the narrowest point. Bend to the side and find the crease.
    High hip
    About 4″ below the waist, across the top of the hip bones.
    Low hip
    Around the fullest part of the seat, feet together.
    Hollow to hem
    From the dip between the collarbones to the floor, measured barefoot.
    Heel height
    The height of your wedding shoes. Added to hollow-to-hem when the hem is cut.
    Between sizes?
    Order to your largest measurement. Taking a gown in is easy; letting it out is often impossible.
